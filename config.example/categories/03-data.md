---
key: data
name: Data Platform
description: Moves, models and governs data so that people, products and AI systems can trust it and use it independently.
---

## Level 1

### Understands and works with data

**Example behaviours:**

- Queries and explores data using basic SQL, validating results before using them.
- Understands where product data originates and how it is consumed.
- Recognises common data quality problems and knows when to raise them.

## Level 2

### Uses data capabilities to solve problems

**Example behaviours:**

- Uses existing data platforms, pipelines and datasets to support product, analytics or AI use cases.
- Uses and validates existing data transformations, adapting straightforward ones when appropriate.
- Applies basic data quality checks and documents the meaning, source and ownership of datasets.
- Understands the purpose and trade-offs of batch processing, streaming and data storage approaches.

## Level 3

### Engineers reliable data systems

**Example behaviours:**

- Designs and operates reliable data pipelines, with appropriate testing, monitoring, recovery and backfill mechanisms.
- Models business domains into reusable, well-defined datasets for analytical and operational consumers.
- Selects appropriate ingestion and processing patterns, including batch, streaming and change data capture.
- Establishes data contracts, quality expectations and lineage with producers and consumers, handling schema evolution safely.

## Level 4

### Architects self-service data platforms

**Example behaviours:**

- Designs reusable platform capabilities that enable teams to produce, discover, transform and consume data independently.
- Establishes common patterns for data modelling, contracts, quality, observability and lifecycle management.
- Makes platform capabilities reliable, cost-efficient and easy to adopt, measuring adoption and operational performance.
- Embeds automated quality checks and governance controls into data workflows, reducing manual coordination across teams.

## Level 5

### Sets the technical direction for data platforms

**Example behaviours:**

- Defines and evolves the long-term technical architecture for data across domains and teams.
- Establishes architectural principles and evaluates major platform investments and technology choices against business needs.
- Guides the evolution of data capabilities for analytics, operational systems and AI workloads.
- Drives organisation-wide technical standards for interoperability, reliability, discoverability and data quality.
