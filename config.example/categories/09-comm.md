key: comm
name: Communication & Influence
description: Shares the right amount of information with the right people, at the right time, listens and influences without authority
---

## Level 1

### Keeps their team informed

**Example behaviours:**

- Gives clear status updates and raises blockers in the team rituals
- Writes pull requests, tickets and messages with enough context for others to understand them

### Listens to understand

**Example behaviours:**

- Listens before talking and asks questions to understand
- Checks that they understood by summarising what they heard

## Level 2

### Contributes to discussions and decisions

**Example behaviours:**

- Prepares meetings, shares their concerns and challenges ideas respectfully
- Writes short documents that explain a problem, the options and a recommendation

## Level 3

### Communicates with stakeholders outside their team

**Example behaviours:**

- Adapts the message and the language to technical and non-technical audiences
- Writes for the reader: the summary first, and the details for those who need them
- Facilitates meetings with a clear agenda, focus and action points

## Level 4

### Influences decisions across teams

**Example behaviours:**

- Builds agreement between teams with different goals, without relying on authority
- Writes proposals and decision records that others use as a reference
- Handles difficult conversations and conflicts calmly and directly

### Represents their area

**Example behaviours:**

- Explains the work and the needs of their area to leadership and to other departments
- Negotiates priorities and resources with other areas

## Level 5

### Communicates clearly company-wide and beyond

**Example behaviours:**

- Uses storytelling to explain the strategy and to make people care about it
- Represents the company at conferences, with customers or in the community
- Is trusted by leadership to communicate difficult news
