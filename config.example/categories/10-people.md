key: people
name: People Leadership
description: Learns from guidance and feedback, helps others grow, builds healthy teams and shapes organisations where people can do their best work
---

## Level 1

### Is coachable and grows from feedback

**Example behaviours:**

- Welcomes guidance and constructive feedback, asks questions to understand it and acts on it.
- Recognises gaps in their knowledge or judgement and asks for help before they become problems.
- Reflects on mistakes and demonstrates changes in behaviour or approach based on what they learn.
- Supports teammates by sharing knowledge, answering questions and helping newcomers settle in.

## Level 2

### Gives feedback and helps others grow

**Example behaviours:**

- Gives specific, respectful and actionable feedback, and remains open to feedback on their own work and behaviour.
- Mentors less experienced colleagues, adapting their explanations and support to the person's needs.
- Pairs and collaborates in ways that build others' confidence and autonomy rather than taking over the work.
- Recognises when a colleague needs support and offers help without assuming authority over them.
- Proactively seeks feedback, recognises personal development areas and applies guidance to become more effective and autonomous.

## Level 3

### Coaches others and strengthens team health

**Example behaviours:**

- Helps colleagues identify development goals, reflect on their work and grow through coaching and feedback, without needing to be their manager.
- Creates opportunities for knowledge sharing, constructive disagreement and learning within the team.
- Recognises early signs of conflict, exclusion, overload or disengagement and helps address them or escalates them appropriately.
- Contributes to fair hiring and assessment through structured interviews, evidence-based feedback or other relevant activities.
- Influences others through trust, example and sound judgement rather than formal authority.
- Remains open to challenge and different perspectives, including feedback from less experienced colleagues, and adjusts their approach when warranted.

## Level 4

### Builds and develops healthy, effective teams

**Example behaviours:**

- Hires, onboards and develops people with complementary skills, setting clear expectations and supporting their growth.
- Holds effective one-to-ones, provides ongoing feedback, manages performance fairly and supports career development.
- Creates a psychologically safe environment where people can challenge decisions, raise concerns and learn from mistakes.
- Makes hiring, performance and promotion decisions consistently, using clear criteria and evidence.
- Develops future leaders, delegates meaningful ownership and adapts team responsibilities as ways of working evolve, including the use of AI agents.
- Actively seeks feedback on their leadership and its impact on others, acknowledges mistakes and changes behaviours when necessary.

## Level 5

### Shapes the organisation and its leadership capability

**Example behaviours:**

- Designs organisational structures, leadership responsibilities and career paths that support strategy, autonomy and sustainable growth.
- Builds leadership capability and succession across teams, coaching managers and senior individual contributors.
- Establishes fair, consistent approaches to talent development, performance, hiring and progression across the organisation.
- Anticipates organisational health and capability risks, and leads significant changes while protecting trust and engagement.
- Evolves workforce and organisational capabilities as technology, including AI agents, changes how work is done.
- Challenges their own assumptions, invites dissenting perspectives and evolves their leadership approach based on feedback and organisational needs.
