name: Delivery
description: Turns goals into results through incremental delivery, effective coordination and continuous improvement of how work gets done
---

## Level 1

### Delivers individual work reliably

**Example behaviours:**

- Breaks assigned work into manageable steps and delivers against agreed expectations.
- Communicates progress, uncertainty and blockers early.
- Takes responsibility for completing and validating their work.
- Asks for help or renegotiates commitments when circumstances change.

## Level 2

### Delivers small initiatives independently

**Example behaviours:**

- Defines scope, dependencies and milestones for small initiatives with clear acceptance criteria.
- Delivers in small, usable increments that enable early feedback.
- Identifies common risks and adjusts plans or scope when new information emerges.
- Balances speed, simplicity and completeness without compromising essential quality.

### Maintains visibility and alignment

**Example behaviours:**

- Communicates progress, risks and changes in expectations to relevant stakeholders.
- Ensures delivered work meets its agreed objectives and completion criteria.

## Level 3

### Drives complex initiatives with others

**Example behaviours:**

- Breaks down complex initiatives into incremental deliverables with clear ownership and dependencies.
- Coordinates with engineering, product, design or other stakeholders to align execution.
- Anticipates delivery risks, resolves dependencies and escalates decisions before they become blockers.
- Adapts scope, sequencing and commitments as new information emerges.

### Improves delivery effectiveness

**Example behaviours:**

- Identifies bottlenecks, unnecessary handoffs and sources of rework in the team's delivery process.
- Uses delivery metrics and feedback to improve predictability, throughput and quality.
- Makes trade-offs visible and helps stakeholders make informed decisions.

## Level 4

### Improves delivery across teams

**Example behaviours:**

- Aligns multiple teams around shared outcomes, milestones and delivery dependencies.
- Manages competing priorities and makes trade-offs between scope, timing, capacity and risk explicit.
- Identifies systemic delivery risks and coordinates their mitigation across teams.
- Maintains stakeholder alignment while adapting plans to changing circumstances.

### Improves the delivery system

**Example behaviours:**

- Establishes lightweight planning, coordination and feedback practices that help teams deliver independently.
- Uses flow, predictability and quality metrics to identify systemic constraints and drive improvements.
- Reduces unnecessary dependencies, handoffs and coordination overhead.
- Improves teams' ability to deliver incrementally and adapt without sacrificing quality or sustainability.

## Level 5

### Shapes organisational delivery effectiveness

**Example behaviours:**

- Establishes operating principles and planning mechanisms that connect strategic priorities with execution across multiple areas.
- Aligns organisational capacity, dependencies and delivery commitments with business priorities.
- Identifies and removes structural constraints that limit the organisation's ability to deliver.
- Establishes organisation-wide mechanisms to assess delivery effectiveness, manage systemic risks and adapt priorities.
- Guides the evolution of delivery practices through growth, technological change and organisational transformation.
