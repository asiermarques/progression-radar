name: Delivery
description: Turns goals into plans and plans into results, delivering in small steps and managing risks and dependencies
---

## Level 1

### Delivers their own tasks reliably

**Example behaviours:**

- Breaks their work into small tasks and gives honest estimates
- Keeps the status of their work visible and raises blockers early

## Level 2

### Delivers small projects

**Example behaviours:**

- Plans a project with interim milestones and hits them
- Considers alternatives and balances pragmatism with polish
- Ships in small increments that can be used and tested

## Level 3

### Delivers projects through a team

**Example behaviours:**

- Scopes projects with product and design, and cuts scope to deliver value early
- Identifies risks and dependencies with other teams and manages them before they become blockers
- Adapts the plan when what is learnt changes the priorities, and tells stakeholders

## Level 4

### Delivers programmes across several teams

**Example behaviours:**

- Coordinates the work of several teams towards a common outcome
- Makes trade-offs between scope, time and quality explicit and gets them agreed
- Improves how teams plan and deliver, so that results are predictable without heavy process

### Manages risks and dependencies across teams

**Example behaviours:**

- Spots conflicting priorities between teams early and gets them resolved
- Tells leadership about risks with options, not only problems

## Level 5

### Shapes how the organisation delivers

**Example behaviours:**

- Sets the planning and delivery rhythm of the organisation and connects it to the company strategy
- Removes structural blockers between departments
- Keeps the organisation delivering through big changes, such as a reorganisation or the adoption of agents
